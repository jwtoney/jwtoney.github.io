Professional Summary

I develop machine learning methods for quantum chemistry, with a focus on the systems conventional models handle worst: open-shell, organometallic, and strongly correlated chemistry. My work spans graph neural networks for metal–ligand coordination, large quantum-chemical datasets across charge and spin states, uncertainty-aware architectures covering elements 1–100, and correlated wavefunction theory on classical and quantum hardware.
